Accurate timekeeping ensures fair compensation for employees and compliance with federal labor laws. Master decimal time conversion and shift duration calculations.

## Key Takeaways
- To convert clock minutes to decimal hours, divide minutes by 60 (e.g., 30 mins = 0.50 hours, 15 mins = 0.25 hours).
- Under US FLSA guidelines, non-exempt employees working over 40 hours in a 7-day workweek must receive 1.5x time-and-a-half pay.
- Mandatory lunch breaks must be subtracted from total daily shift durations before calculating weekly overtime thresholds.

## 1. Converting Clock Time to Decimal Hours for Payroll

Payroll processing systems operate on decimal numbers rather than sexagesimal (base-60) clock hours and minutes.

Common minute-to-decimal conversions:
- 15 minutes = 0.25 hours
- 30 minutes = 0.50 hours
- 45 minutes = 0.75 hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does holiday or vacation pay count toward 40 overtime hours?
No. Under federal FLSA guidelines, only actual physical hours worked count toward the 40-hour weekly threshold for overtime calculation.
