# Optimizing Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) with Image Compression

Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) is a core web vitals metric measuring page load speed. Hero images are often the primary cause of poor LCP scores, making compression essential.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Optimize LCP by compressing hero images using WebP, setting explicit width/height tags to prevent layout shifts, and preloading critical assets.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good LCP score?
To provide a good user experience, websites should strive to have a Largest Contentful Paint of **2.5 seconds or less** for at least 75% of page visits.

### How does image compression help SEO?
Faster loading pages rank higher in Google search results and reduce user bounce rates.
