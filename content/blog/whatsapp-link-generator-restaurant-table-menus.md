# WhatsApp Link Generator: Restaurant Table Menus

Deploying campaigns across ad networks requires custom tracking links. Here is how parameters are structured.

A **WhatsApp link builder** is a marketing utility that formats phone numbers with country prefixes into direct wa.me chat URLs. This enables users to start an instant chat without saving numbers.

For modern dining spaces, cafes, and hotels, offering contactless services improves order efficiency. ## 📐 Formatting Table QR Menu Systems

To deploy contactless restaurant ordering systems:
1.  **Format Pre-Filled Messages**: Set templates to identify table numbers (e.g. `https://wa.me/15550192834?text=Hi!%20I%20am%20at%20table%205%20and%20would%20like%20to%20view%20the%20menu`).
2.  **Generate Crisp Vectors**: Create high-quality QR matrices to ensure printed cards scan easily under low restaurant lighting.
3.  **Deploy Automated Menus**: Set up automated auto-replies or catalog features on your WhatsApp Business account to send menu PDFs instantly.
