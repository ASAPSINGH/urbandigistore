# Optimizing Meta Title & Description Length for SEO CTR

Search engines truncate titles and descriptions that exceed pixel limits, which can lower click-through rates. Staying within recommended character counts is key to search snippet optimization.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Keep meta titles under 60 characters (600 pixels) and meta descriptions under 160 characters (960 pixels) to prevent truncation in Google search results.

## 📊 Metadata Length Limits Guidelines

| Tag Type | Recommended Character Count | Maximum Pixel Width | Truncation Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta Title** | 50 - 60 chars | 600 px | Vague titles, lower CTR |
| **Meta Description** | 120 - 160 chars | 960 px | Cut-off sentences |
| **H1 Tag** | 20 - 70 chars | *No limit* | Poor page structure |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Google use meta descriptions for ranking?
No, meta descriptions are not a direct ranking factor. However, a compelling description increases search CTR, which can positively impact rankings.

### Why did Google rewrite my search title?
Google may rewrite titles that are too long, stuffed with keywords, or fail to accurately reflect the page content.
