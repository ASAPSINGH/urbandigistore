# Position Sizing in Crypto: Managing Leverage and Risk

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile, making risk management essential. Understanding how to calculate position size and risk allocation is key to avoiding liquidation.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Crypto risk management requires setting your stop loss first, and calculating position size based on account risk: Position Size = (Account Balance × Risk %) / Stop Loss %.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Does higher leverage increase my risk?
Only if you increase your position size. If your position size remains constant, higher leverage simply reduces the capital required to open the trade.

### What is the recommended risk per trade?
Most professional risk management models suggest risking **1% to 2%** of your total account equity on a single trade.
