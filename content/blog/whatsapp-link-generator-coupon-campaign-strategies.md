## 📐 Formatting Coupon Codes inside wa.me Links

To deploy click-to-chat coupon promotions:
1.  **Format Coupon Templates**: Pre-fill your message templates with specific promotional codes (e.g. `https://wa.me/15550192834?text=I%20want%20to%20redeem%20coupon%20CHAT30`).
2.  **Add Campaign Parameters**: Use source and medium tags (e.g. `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio`) to identify which social channels are driving your promotions.
3.  **Deploy Call-to-Action Buttons**: Set up prominent promo links in your Instagram bio, TikTok profiles, or email campaign templates.
