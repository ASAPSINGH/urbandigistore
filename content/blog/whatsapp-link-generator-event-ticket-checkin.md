## 📐 Formatting Ticket Check-In Systems

To deploy contactless check-in systems:
1.  **Format Pre-Filled Messages**: Set templates to identify ticket numbers (e.g. `https://wa.me/15550192834?text=Hi!%20My%20ticket%20ID%20is%20TCK-9281%20and%20I%20would%20like%20to%20check%20in`).
2.  **Generate Crisp Vectors**: Create high-quality QR matrices to ensure printed tickets scan easily under dim event hall lighting.
3.  **Deploy Auto-Response Rules**: Set up quick replies on your WhatsApp Business account to send venue maps or seating arrangements instantly.
