# Social Media Aspect Ratios & Image Cropping Guides

Publishing images with incorrect aspect ratios leads to automatic cropping or black filler bars, decreasing engagement. Maintaining platform-specific ratios is essential for premium brand presentation.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between an image's width and height. For Instagram posts, use a 1:1 square ratio (1080x1080px); for stories and Reels, use a 9:16 vertical ratio (1080x1920px).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does Instagram compress my high-res photos?
Instagram automatically downsizes images larger than 1080px wide. Pre-cropping to 1080px prevents the platform's compression engine from introducing artifacts.

### Does cropping reduce image resolution?
Yes, cropping discards pixels outside the selection. Using high-resolution source photos prevents blurry outputs.
