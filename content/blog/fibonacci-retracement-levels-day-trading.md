# Applying Fibonacci Retracement Levels in Day Trading

Fibonacci retracements are a popular tool used by technical analysts to identify support and resistance levels. These levels are derived from the mathematical golden ratio.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Fibonacci retracement levels are calculated by taking the distance between a market high and low and dividing it by key Fibonacci ratios: 23.6%, 38.2%, 50.0%, 61.8%, and 78.6%.

Traders use the inverse ratios to estimate price retracements:
*   \(1 / 1.618 = 0.618\) (The 61.8% Retracement)
*   \(1 / 2.618 = 0.382\) (The 38.2% Retracement)

When a market is trending, prices often pull back to these mathematical levels before resuming their primary trend.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Fibonacci level is the most important?
The **61.8%** and **38.2%** levels are widely considered the most important retracement levels in financial markets.

### Do Fibonacci levels work in all markets?
Yes. Fibonacci retracements are used across stocks, forex, indices, and cryptocurrency markets.
