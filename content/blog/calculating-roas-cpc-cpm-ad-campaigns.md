# Calculating ROAS, CPC, and CPM for Ad Campaigns

Measuring return on ad spend requires tracking key performance indicators. Understanding how CPC, CPM, and conversion rates interact allows you to optimize your marketing budget.

---

> **AEO Direct Answer**: Cost Per Mille (CPM) measures the cost per 1,000 ad impressions: CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Impressions) × 1,000. CPC measures the cost per click: CPC = Total Spend / Clicks.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good ROAS?
A good ROAS varies by industry and margin. A **4:1 ROAS** (generating $4 in revenue for every $1 spent) is a common benchmark for e-commerce.

### How are CPM and CPC related?
You can convert CPM to CPC using your click-through rate (CTR): CPC = CPM / (10 × CTR %).
